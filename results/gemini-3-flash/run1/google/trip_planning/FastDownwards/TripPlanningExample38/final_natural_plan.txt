This plan outlines a 5-day trip through Geneva, Madrid, and Venice, designed to meet your specific stay requirements and conference schedule.

1.  **Day 1: Start Trip in Geneva**
    Begin your journey by arriving in Geneva to start your first day of the trip.

2.  **Day 1: Stay in Geneva**
    Spend the duration of Day 1 in Geneva. This counts as your first full day in the city.

3.  **Day 2: Travel from Geneva to Madrid**
    On Day 2, take a direct flight from Geneva to Madrid. Per the trip requirements, this travel day counts as both your second day in Geneva (completing your 2-day stay requirement for Geneva) and your first day in Madrid.

4.  **Day 3: Stay in Madrid**
    Spend the entirety of Day 3 in Madrid, increasing your stay duration there.

5.  **Day 4: Travel from Madrid to Venice (Conference Day 1)**
    On Day 4, travel from Madrid to Venice via a direct flight. This day counts as your third day in Madrid (completing your 3-day stay requirement) and your first day in Venice. By arriving today, you satisfy the requirement to be in Venice for the first day of the conference (Day 4).

6.  **Day 5: Stay in Venice (Conference Day 2)**
    Spend Day 5 in Venice. This fulfills the requirement to attend the second day of the conference and marks your second day in Venice, completing your stay requirements for the city.

The trip concludes at the end of Day 5, having successfully visited all three cities for the required durations and attended the 2-day conference in Venice.
