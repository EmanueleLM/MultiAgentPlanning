To achieve the goal of rearranging the crates as specified, please follow these instructions:

**Step 1:** Drive **truck2** from **depot2** to **depot1**.

**Step 2:** At **distributor0**, use **hoist3** to lift **crate2** from the top of **crate0**.

**Step 3:** Drive **truck2** from **depot1** to **distributor0**.

**Step 4:** At **distributor0**, use **hoist3** to load **crate2** into **truck2**.

**Step 5:** At **distributor0**, use **hoist3** to lift **crate0** from **pallet3**.

**Step 6:** At **distributor0**, use **hoist3** to load **crate0** into **truck2**.

**Step 7:** Drive **truck2** from **distributor0** to **depot1**.

**Step 8:** At **depot1**, use **hoist1** to lift **crate1** from **pallet1**.

**Step 9:** At **depot1**, use **hoist1** to load **crate1** into **truck2**.

**Step 10:** Drive **truck2** from **depot1** to **depot0**.

**Step 11:** At **depot0**, use **hoist0** to unload **crate1** from **truck2**.

**Step 12:** At **depot0**, use **hoist0** to drop **crate1** onto **pallet0**.

**Step 13:** At **depot0**, use **hoist0** to unload **crate2** from **truck2**. (The hoist will hold this crate for now).

**Step 14:** Drive **truck2** from **depot0** to **depot2**.

**Step 15:** At **depot2**, use **hoist2** to unload **crate0** from **truck2**.

**Step 16:** At **depot2**, use **hoist2** to drop **crate0** onto **pallet2**.

**Step 17:** Back at **depot0**, use **hoist0** to drop **crate2** onto the top of **crate1**.

Following these steps will result in crate0 being on pallet2, crate1 being on pallet0, and crate2 being on crate1.
