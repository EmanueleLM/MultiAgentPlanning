This logistics plan outlines the steps required to transport packages across two cities using trucks for local transport and an airplane for inter-city transit.

**Step 1: Load Package 3**
In City 1, load **package_3** into **truck_1** at **location_1_1**.

**Step 2: Move Truck 1 to Location 1-0**
Drive **truck_1** from **location_1_1** to **location_1_0** (the airport in City 1).

**Step 3: Move Truck 1 to Location 1-2**
Drive **truck_1** from **location_1_0** to **location_1_2** in City 1.

**Step 4: Load Package 2**
Load **package_2** into **truck_1** at **location_1_2**.

**Step 5: Return Truck 1 to Airport**
Drive **truck_1** from **location_1_2** back to **location_1_0** in City 1.

**Step 6: Unload Package 3 at City 1 Airport**
Unload **package_3** from **truck_1** at **location_1_0**.

**Step 7: Unload Package 2 at City 1 Airport**
Unload **package_2** from **truck_1** at **location_1_0**.

**Step 8: Move Truck 0 to Pickup Point**
In City 0, drive **truck_0** from **location_0_0** to **location_0_1**.

**Step 9: Load Package 4**
Load **package_4** into **truck_0** at **location_0_1**.

**Step 10: Return Truck 0 to City 0 Airport**
Drive **truck_0** from **location_0_1** back to **location_0_0**.

**Step 11: Unload Package 4 at City 0 Airport**
Unload **package_4** from **truck_0** at **location_0_0**.

**Step 12: Airplane Transit to City 1**
Fly **airplane_0** from **location_0_0** (City 0 airport) to **location_1_0** (City 1 airport).

**Step 13: Load Package 3 for Transport**
Load **package_3** into **airplane_0** at **location_1_0**.

**Step 14: Load Package 2 for Transport**
Load **package_2** into **airplane_0** at **location_1_0**.

**Step 15: Load Package 0 for Transport**
Load **package_0** into **airplane_0** at **location_1_0**.

**Step 16: Airplane Transit to City 0**
Fly **airplane_0** from **location_1_0** back to **location_0_0**.

**Step 17: Load Package 4 into Airplane**
Load **package_4** into **airplane_0** at **location_0_0**.

**Step 18: Deliver Package 3**
Unload **package_3** from **airplane_0** at **location_0_0**. (Goal for package_3 met).

**Step 19: Unload Package 2 for local delivery**
Unload **package_2** from **airplane_0** at **location_0_0**.

**Step 20: Load Package 2 for local transport**
Load **package_2** into **truck_0** at **location_0_0**.

**Step 21: Local delivery of Package 2**
Drive **truck_0** from **location_0_0** to **location_0_2**.

**Step 22: Unload Package 2 at destination**
Unload **package_2** from **truck_0** at **location_0_2**. (Goal for package_2 met).

**Step 23: Deliver Package 0**
Unload **package_0** from **airplane_0** at **location_0_0**. (Goal for package_0 met).

**Step 24: Final Airplane Transit to City 1**
Fly **airplane_0** from **location_0_0** back to **location_1_0**.

**Step 25: Deliver Package 4**
Unload **package_4** from **airplane_0** at **location_1_0**. (Goal for package_4 met).

*Note: Package 1 and Package 5 were already at their target locations (location_1_1 and location_0_1, respectively) at the start of the operations.*
