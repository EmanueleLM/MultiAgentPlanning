Here is the human-readable sequence of actions you need to complete the Sokoban puzzle:

1. You (the player) start at position 3, where you need to push the box.
2. You see a box at position 2 and the goal is at position 1.
3. The task is to push the box from position 2 to position 1.

   **Step 1:** 
   - Action: Push the box from position 2 to position 1.
   - Outcome: The box moves into the goal position.
   - Cost: This action is performed with a unit cost.

By following the above step, the box will be successfully moved into the goal position, achieving the task's completion.
