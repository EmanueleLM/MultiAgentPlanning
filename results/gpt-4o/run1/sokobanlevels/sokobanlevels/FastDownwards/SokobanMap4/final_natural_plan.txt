To solve the Sokoban problem as specified, follow these steps:

1. **Initial Setup**: 
   - You (the player) start at position p5.
   - The box is initially located at position p3.
   - The goal (where the box needs to be placed) is located at position p1.

2. **Objective**:
   - Move the box from its initial position p3 to the goal position p1.

3. **Action Plan**:
   - **Action**: Push the box from position p3 to position p1.
     - **Context**: You start at position p5.
     - You move next to the box located at p3, which is adjacent to you.
     - Push the box from p3 to its goal at p1, which is adjacent to p3.
     - After the push, you occupy the position p3 where the box initially was.
     - **Outcome**: The box now occupies the goal position at p1, thus solving the puzzle.

This single action achieves the goal of moving the box to coincide with the goal position on the Sokoban map.
