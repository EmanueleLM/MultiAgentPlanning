To solve this Sokoban problem, follow these steps:

1. **Move Player**
   - Move the player from position 1 to position 2.

2. **Push Box**
   - Push the box from position 48 to position 96. While pushing the box, the player moves from position 2 to position 48 to complete the action.

This completes the task sequence required to achieve the goal of having the box at position 96. Every action is intended to take one unit of time.
