1. **Day 1:** Start the trip in **Dubrovnik**.  
2. **Day 1 → Day 2:** Take a direct flight from **Dubrovnik** to **Oslo**.  
3. **Day 2 → Day 3:** Take a direct flight from **Oslo** to **Dubrovnik**.  
4. **Day 3 → Day 4:** Take a direct flight from **Dubrovnik** to **Oslo**.  
5. **Day 4 → Day 5:** **Stay in Oslo** for one day.  
6. **Day 5 → Day 6:** Take a direct flight from **Oslo** to **Porto**.  
7. **Day 6 → Day 7:** Take a direct flight from **Porto** to **Oslo**.  
8. **Day 7 → Day 8:** Take a direct flight from **Oslo** to **Dubrovnik**.  
9. **Day 8 → Day 9:** Take a direct flight from **Dubrovnik** to **Oslo**.  
10. **Day 9 → Day 10:** **Stay in Oslo** for one day.  
11. **Day 10 → Day 11:** Take a direct flight from **Oslo** to **Dubrovnik**.  
12. **Day 11 → Day 12:** **Stay in Dubrovnik** for one day, so you are in Dubrovnik on **day 12** for the conference.  
13. **Day 12 → Day 13:** Take a direct flight from **Dubrovnik** to **Oslo**.  
14. **Day 13 → Day 14:** Take a direct flight from **Oslo** to **Porto**.  
15. **Day 14 → Day 15:** Take a direct flight from **Porto** to **Oslo**.  
16. **Day 15 → Day 16:** Take a direct flight from **Oslo** to **Dubrovnik**, arriving in **Dubrovnik on day 16** for the conference.
